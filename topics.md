# Research Topics

---

## Topic Structure

Each piece takes one **main topic** as its primary focus — something the owner wants explored and understood. **Related topics** are surfaced automatically during research sessions; they sit in a holding area until the owner confirms them for the main list.

A related topic may appear as a secondary angle within a piece before being confirmed, but must be flagged for review. It joins the main list only when the owner approves.

*Note: topics suggested by the owner may be interpreted broadly — the most interesting angle on a subject matters more than a literal treatment of the label.*

## Main Topics

- **Childhood emotional neglect** — what it is and how it differs from active abuse; how absence of attunement shapes adults; the quiet damage of what didn't happen and the difficulty of naming it
- **Childhood emotional neglect: nervous system effects** — how early relational absence shapes the developing autonomic nervous system; polyvagal theory and the window of tolerance; dysregulation patterns (freeze, fawn, hypervigilance) that originate in emotional neglect rather than active trauma; interoception deficits — the difficulty reading your own body's signals; the physiology of being chronically unseen; what nervous system repair actually looks like
- **IT automation** — the landscape of automation across corporate and home IT environments, with and without AI; what gets handed to machines, what resists it, and what the difference means in practice
- **UK IT job market** — where the industry is heading; which roles are growing or contracting; what skills are worth building now; the market as it actually exists for people working in it
- **Dungeon Mastering** — the craft of running D&D; worldbuilding, encounter design, managing a table, improvising when players go off-script; the DM as author, referee, and performer at once
- **Sewing your own clothes** — getting started with practical garment construction; patterns, fabric, fit, tools, and the satisfaction of wearing something you made
- **Crossdressing and presentation** — practical guidance on how males can dress and present femininely and look good doing it; fit, proportion, styling, makeup, and building confidence in a new presentation
- **Jungian psychology** — the core ideas: archetypes, the shadow, anima/animus, individuation, the collective unconscious; what Jung was actually saying and why it still resonates
- **Jungian psychology: identity and sexuality** — Jung's framework as it applies specifically to gender identity, sexual orientation, and becoming who you are; the anima, the persona, and the parts of the self that get suppressed or buried
- **Reading sheet music** — the practical skill of translating notation into sound; how to build fluency gradually, what the symbols mean, how rhythm and pitch notation work, and how to stop being afraid of the page
- **Interior decoration** — making a living space feel considered and good; furniture styles that work together, colour and light, proportion and clutter, what to spend on and what doesn't matter
- **Microsoft tenant admin updates** — recent developments in Microsoft 365, Entra ID, Intune, and the admin centre that matter to tenant administrators; new features, deprecations, policy changes, and things worth knowing
- **Everyday fashion** — how to put together outfits that look coordinated and intentional without being a fashion expert; colour and pattern matching, building a wardrobe with pieces that work together; fabric knowledge including how different materials feel to wear, how they behave in rain, heat, and humidity, how they pick up and resist stains, and how to wash and care for them properly so they last
- **Rich brown and burgundy colour schemes** — how to build rooms and outfits around deep browns and burgundy/wine tones; what colours, textures, and materials they pair with; how lighting affects these shades; applying the palette across both interior decoration and clothing
- **Azure service principals and gMSA** — what service principals are and how they work in Azure/Entra ID; managed identities vs service principals; Group Managed Service Accounts (gMSA): what they solve, how they work on Windows and in hybrid/cloud environments, and when to use each
- **Mending clothes: seams and worn fabric** — how to re-stitch a seam that has come apart cleanly; how to repair fabric that has worn through (including high-friction areas like trouser crotches and inner thighs); patching techniques, darning, and when to reinforce before the hole appears; practical home repair vs when a tailor is worth it
- **DHCP lease and scope management** — ideal lease duration and scope settings for a range of site sizes and device densities; how to manage a subnet that can't grow as a site gets progressively busier; the tradeoffs between short vs long leases, address exhaustion, and client experience; practical strategies for squeezing more from a fixed IP range without renumbering; when scope options, exclusions, and reservations help and when they don't
- **Docker and containers** — what containers are and why they exist; how Docker works; images, containers, volumes, and networks; Dockerfile basics; Docker Compose for multi-container setups; how containers differ from VMs; where they fit in a modern deployment pipeline
- **AI automation in large organisations** — practical uses of AI and agentic automation in enterprise IT: service desk triage, patch management, onboarding/offboarding, monitoring and alerting, compliance checking, documentation generation; what large orgs are actually deploying now vs what's still experimental; governance and oversight requirements at scale
- **AI qualifications and certifications** — officially recognised courses and credentials in AI and machine learning; vendor certifications (Microsoft AI-900, Azure AI Engineer, Google Cloud, AWS); academic and professional body qualifications; what carries genuine weight with employers vs what is marketing; where to start depending on technical background
- **Self-hosted AI for organisations** — how to deploy and run a large language model entirely on internal infrastructure so sensitive company data never leaves the building; open-weight models (Llama, Mistral, Phi) and what hardware they need; RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) for feeding company documents, processes, and knowledge bases to the model; tooling options (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM, Open WebUI); access control and employee-facing interfaces; what this realistically costs and what capability it delivers vs a cloud API
- **Childhood emotional neglect: how to handle the relationship and memories with parents** - wahat the reccomended pproaches for dealing with the relationship with the CEN victim's parents; both their continues relationship in the modern day, as well as their shifting feelings towards the past with their parents.
- **Freudian psychosexual development and childhood emotional neglect** — how Freud's stage model (oral, anal, phallic, latency, genital) maps onto cases of CEN; where early relational absence causes fixation or arrested development; the significance of the "quiet infant who didn't cry" as a potential marker of very early learned helplessness or shutdown — emotional suppression that begins before language; what this early onset means for character structure and adult presentation
